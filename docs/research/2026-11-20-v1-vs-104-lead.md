# v1.0 vs 1.04 pathfinding lead

- Date: 2026-11-20
- Task: T-113 (community lead investigation)
- Status: **unverified**; no v1.0 performance claim
- Related: [external pathfinding research](2026-11-20-external-pathfinding-research.md)

## Why this is open

Community threads consistently report that late-game cliff/large-map pathfinding
is better on v1.0 than on patched 1.04. The common “downgrade to v1.0” fix is
anecdotal but repeated. If true, part of the Last Stand cost could be a 1.04
regression rather than an inherent SAGE limit.

## What can be verified locally

[VERIFIED] The workspace has three relevant binaries:

| Binary | Bytes | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| `game/work/game.dat` | 6,883,376 | patched 1.04, SHA-256 `F37A4929...495B` |
| `game/work/game.dat.bak` | 6,483,968 | pre-patch backup; no PE version resource |
| `game/work/generals.exe.bak` | 6,480,431 | pre-patch backup; no PE version resource |

[VERIFIED] The `.bak` files contain no usable file/product version string; the
generic “1.0” strings are not a version resource. Their timestamps
(2005-03-10 and 2006-02-17) and ~400 KB smaller size are consistent with a
pre-patch binary, but the exact 1.0/1.03/1.04 identity is `[UNKNOWN]`.

[VERIFIED] The GPL source in this project is the TheSuperHackers 1.04 lineage;
it contains the 30k retail pathfinding pool, `RETAIL_COMPATIBLE_PATHFINDING`
and the 2026 per-cell allocation/failover work. A v1.0 binary would have to be
compared by behavior or disassembly; the 1.0 source is not available here.

## What a valid comparison needs

1. Confirm the `.bak` identity (PE timestamps, imported functions, strings,
   behavior) or obtain a known v1.0 executable.
2. Use a **stock scenario** that both versions can run: a stock map, a stock
   skirmish or a stock replay recorded on the matching version.
3. Measure the same clock-aligned window: frame time, logic time, path-search
   time, unit counts, failed paths and completed orders.
4. Keep the mod out of the comparison; ShockWave is data-only, but its replay
   is 1.04/diagnostic-specific and cannot prove an engine difference.

The existing `ZH_MAP`/`ZH_STRESS` harness is compiled only into the rebuilt
source, not the retail v1.0 binary; a v1.0 run needs a UI-started stock
skirmish or a v1.0-compatible replay.

## Current conclusion

[INFERRED] The community lead is plausible but **not established**. The 1.04
source and the 2026 pathfinding fixes suggest that some of the reported cliff
behavior is a known retail resource/pool issue, not necessarily a v1.0
algorithmic advantage. Do not cite v1.0 as a fix until a stock A/B is recorded.
